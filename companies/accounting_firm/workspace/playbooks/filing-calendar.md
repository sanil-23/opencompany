# Filing calendar

The dates this firm cannot move. The `filings` ledger holds the rows; this note
says how the calendar is kept, because a calendar nobody maintains is worse than
none — it is a calendar people trust.

- **Every date has a named authority.** A filing with no authority behind it is
  a habit somebody inherited, and it is worth finding out whether it is still
  required.
- **The penalty goes on the row.** It is what decides whether a filing outranks
  everything else today, and it is the field most often left blank.
- **Prepared is not filed.** A row sits in `awaiting_signature` until a person
  signs. Chase those; nothing there moves on its own.
- **Read it at the start of the session,** not when something feels due.
