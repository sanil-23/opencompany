# Forecast discipline

A stage is a claim about what has been proved, not about how the last call felt.

1. **Economic buyer, named.** Not the champion. If nobody can name them, the
   deal is in discovery whatever the row says — this is the commonest late-stage
   stall there is.
2. **A close date has a sequence behind it.** What happens between now and then,
   and who does each thing. A date with no sequence is a guess with a calendar
   entry.
3. **Procurement and security review are part of the date,** not an unlucky
   surprise afterwards. They are why enterprise deals close in quarters.
4. **Write what would kill it.** If the answer is "nothing", it has not been
   qualified.
5. **A date moves once, deliberately, with a reason.** Twice quietly means it
   was never real.
6. **`no_decision` is a legitimate outcome** and the commonest one. Recording it
   as `lost` hides that the competitor was inertia, which is a different problem
   with a different fix.
