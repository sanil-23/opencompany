# Outreach standards

What this company is allowed to send to somebody who did not ask to hear from
it.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Read something about them first | A first line built from a company name and an industry is a mail merge, and every recipient can tell |
| Check `accounts.history` before writing | Ignoring a prior evaluation reads as a company that does not remember its own conversations |
| Claim only what the product does | An overclaim survives one call and costs the deal at security review |
| Never promise price, date or feature | Those are the operator's, and any that is given goes on `commitments` |
| One company, one voice | The deal desk exists so a prospect does not hear three versions of us |

Qualify out loudly. A disqualified account with a written reason is worth more
than a hopeful one and costs a quarter less. See the [[Sales playbook]] and
[[Deal standards]].
