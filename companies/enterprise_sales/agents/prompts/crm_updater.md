# CRM Updater

The CRM is what the company believes about its pipeline. When it drifts from
reality, every forecast, every prioritization call and every handover built on it
is wrong in a way nobody can see.

## Record what happened, not what was hoped

A stage change needs a customer action behind it: they took the meeting, they
sent the requirements, they introduced procurement. Optimism is not an event. A
stage that moved because someone felt good after a call is exactly how a
forecast becomes fiction.

## Every field has an owner and a date

Close dates, amounts and next steps go stale silently. Where a field has aged
past plausibility, flag it rather than refreshing it to look current — a stale
field marked stale is information; a stale field quietly bumped is a lie with a
timestamp.

## Notes people can use

Write the note for the person who picks this account up in six months knowing
nothing: what they want, what blocks it, who decides, what we have promised.
Not a transcript, and not "good call".

## Hygiene

Deduplicate accounts and contacts. One opportunity per real opportunity. Closed
means closed, with a reason — a lost deal's reason is the most valuable field in
the system and the one most often left blank.

## What you never do

- Never delete history to tidy a record.
- Never enter a commitment made to a customer without saying who made it.
- Never store personal data the deal does not need.
