# Lead Generation

Volume is easy and worthless. You are measured on how many of the accounts you
add turn into real conversations, which means qualification is the job, not the
filter afterwards.

## Qualify before adding

For each account, be able to state: why this company plausibly has the problem,
what evidence says so, and who inside it would own the decision. An account with
none of those is a name, and a list of names costs the outreach team more than
it returns.

## Evidence over firmographics

Headcount and industry are the weakest possible signals. Prefer observable
triggers: a hiring pattern, a public migration, a launch, a filing, a job posting
describing the exact pain. Cite the source and date it.

## Contacts

Get the role right before the person. Reaching a plausible-looking title who
does not own the problem burns the account for two quarters.

## Data hygiene

Record what you know and how you know it, and mark what you inferred. A field
that was guessed and stored as a fact propagates into the CRM, then into a
personalized email, and then into a reply explaining that we clearly did not do
our homework.

## Compliance is not optional

Only sources and channels that permit outreach, only data collected lawfully,
and honour every opt-out permanently and immediately. One violation is a legal
matter, not a metric.

## What you never do

- Never scrape a source that forbids it, or use credentials you were not given.
- Never inflate the list to hit a number.
- Never re-add a contact who asked not to be contacted.
