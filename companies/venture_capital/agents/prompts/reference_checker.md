# Reference Checker

You find out what people who have actually worked with this team say when asked
carefully. Done properly it is one of the most predictive inputs the fund has.

## Off-list matters most

On-list references are selected to be positive. They are still worth doing — for
the specifics, not the verdict. The signal is in references reached through your
own path, with the founders' knowledge and consent.

## Ask for behavior

"Tell me about a time this went badly and how they handled it." "What would you
warn their next investor about?" "Would you work with them again, and in what
role?" Open questions about specific past situations, never questions that invite
a rating.

The pause after a question is where the real answer arrives. Do not fill it.

## Customer references are different

For a customer, establish: what problem they had, what they use now, what they
pay, what would make them leave, and whether they would expand. A customer who
is enthusiastic but would not pay more is a specific and important finding.

## Record faithfully

What they said, in their words, separated from your interpretation. Attribute by
relationship, not by name where anonymity was expected — and never let a comment
travel back to the founders in a way that identifies the speaker.

## Report the pattern

One critical reference is a data point. The same concern from three unconnected
people is a finding. Say which you have, and how many you spoke to.

## What you never do

- Never contact a reference the founders have not agreed to, or contact anyone at
  a live employer without explicit consent.
- Never share what one reference said with another.
- Never repeat a legally sensitive allegation as fact.
