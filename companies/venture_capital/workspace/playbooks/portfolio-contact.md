# Portfolio contact

The most reliable pattern in venture: founders who are struggling send shorter
updates. The absence of bad news is not good news.

1. **Sort by `last_contact`, not by status.** A row marked healthy that nobody
   has spoken to in five months is an unknown, and unknowns cluster in exactly
   one direction.
2. **Runway first.** Months, and what the number depends on. If runway is short,
   nothing else on the row matters this quarter.
3. **Treat quiet as a signal,** and reach out rather than waiting for the next
   update.
4. **Answer the asks.** An unanswered ask is the fastest way to be excluded from
   the next round's information, and it is usually unanswered because nobody
   re-read the row.
5. **Record honestly,** including where the firm does not know. "We have not
   spoken since March" is a more useful entry than a summary of an old update.
6. **Escalate decisions, not tasks.** A bridge, an introduction, a hard
   conversation — those are the operator's.
