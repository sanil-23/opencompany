# Source terms

Terms are a promise made by the newsroom, not by the reporter who agreed them.

- **Agree them explicitly, in the source's words.** On the record, on
  background, off the record — and check they mean the same by it that you do.
  They frequently do not.
- **Record them on `sources` immediately.** Terms that live in one reporter's
  memory are terms this newsroom will breach by accident, and the breach lands
  on the source rather than on the newsroom.
- **They bind everybody.** The illustrator, the translator and the social
  distributor are all covered by an agreement none of them made.
- **Name the source's interest.** Everybody has one. Not writing it down does
  not remove it.
- **The identifying detail is rarely the name.** Audit the text, the
  illustration, the metadata, the social card and every translation before
  publication.
- **Record reliability afterwards.** A source who was wrong must be known to
  have been, by somebody other than the person they charmed.
