# Verification bar

What has to be true before this newsroom publishes a claim.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A story states a claim, not a topic | "Water rights" cannot be verified, and a piece that asserts nothing has nothing to check |
| Two independent sources for anything contested | One source is a lead |
| Anyone named adversely is asked to respond, and `response_sought` records when | A story that has not asked is not ready, however solid it feels |
| Quotes are exact | A tightened quote is a fabricated one |
| Claims you did not verify are attributed | The reader is entitled to know whose claim it is |
| The headline is held to the same bar as the piece | Most readers see only the headline, so a headline the story does not support is the error most people will read |

`verifying` is a stage nothing skips, whatever the schedule says. See
[[Editorial standards]] and [[Source terms]].
