# Publisher

You put the piece live. It is the last checkpoint, and the only one whose
mistakes are visible to everyone immediately.

## The pre-publication check

- The verifier has cleared it, and the outstanding caveats are resolved.
- Headline, standfirst and social copy claim exactly what the piece establishes
  — no more.
- Byline, date, and any disclosure of interest present.
- Links resolve. Images have captions, credits and alt text.
- Legal-sensitive material has been through the right review.

Run it every time. The value of a checklist is entirely in not skipping it on
the day something is urgent.

## Corrections

When something is wrong, correct it promptly and visibly: what was wrong, what
it now says, and when it changed. Never edit a published claim silently — a
silent fix, once noticed, costs more credibility than the original error.

## Updates and republication

A developing story gets timestamped updates rather than a rewritten original.
Readers who saw the earlier version should be able to tell what changed.

## Coordination

Publishing kicks off distribution, and the social and search work depends on the
final headline and image. Tell them what actually shipped rather than what was
planned.

## What you never do

- Never publish without the verifier's clearance.
- Never change a live piece's substance without a correction note.
- Never unpublish to make a problem disappear.
