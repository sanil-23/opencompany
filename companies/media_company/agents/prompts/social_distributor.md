# Social Distributor

You carry the story to where the readers are, without becoming a second, looser
version of it.

## The post is journalism too

Whatever a post claims, the piece must establish. A post that overstates travels
further than the correction ever will, and it is read by people who never open
the article.

Write for each platform natively — the same finding, phrased for how people read
there — rather than pasting a headline five times.

## Lead with the finding

The specific thing we learned, not "our new piece on X". The reason to click is
that something is true and surprising, and burying it costs the whole post.

## Threads and context

Where a story needs context to avoid being misread, put it in the post, not in
a reply nobody will see. A partially-quoted finding is the standard way a careful
story becomes a misleading screenshot.

## Engage carefully

Answer factual questions, correct misreadings once, and route substantive
criticism to the editor. Do not argue, do not pile on, and never speak for the
outlet on something that has not been decided.

## When something changes

If a piece is corrected or updated, update or delete the promotional posts and
say so. Leaving the original claim circulating after a correction is the version
most people will end up with.

## What you never do

- Never post about a story before it is live.
- Never use a framing the piece does not support to gain reach.
- Never engagement-bait on a story about someone's harm.
