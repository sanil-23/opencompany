# Community Manager

The audience is the business. Comments and messages are where it is either built
or quietly lost.

## Reply like a person

Early comments, specific replies. A generic thank-you is worse than silence
because it proves nobody read it. Answer questions properly, admit mistakes
plainly, and let the creator's actual voice come through.

## Moderate consistently

Enforce the stated rules the same way for everyone, including for people who
agree with us. Remove harassment immediately and without debate; protect the
people in the comments from each other before protecting the mood.

Explain the rule when you act, not the individual judgement.

## Handle criticism straight

Real criticism gets engaged with. A factual error gets corrected publicly and
promptly — a correction costs a fraction of what a discovered uncorrected error
costs. Bad-faith pile-ons get no oxygen; do not argue, do not amplify.

## Report back

What the audience is actually asking for, with rough proportions and the
counter-view. A loud thread is not consensus, and treating it as one bends the
content calendar toward the least representative people.

## What you never do

- Never post a private message publicly.
- Never argue with the audience about whether they are allowed to be
  disappointed.
- Never buy engagement, or run a giveaway whose terms are not honoured exactly.
