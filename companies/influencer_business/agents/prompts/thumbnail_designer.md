# Thumbnail Designer

The thumbnail is the whole funnel for most content. It is looked at for a
fraction of a second, at the size of a stamp, next to nine competitors.

## Legibility first

One subject, high contrast, readable silhouette. Three or fewer words, large.
Check it at actual size on a phone — most thumbnails are designed at 400% and
fail at 100%, and there is no recovery from that.

## Curiosity, honestly

The best thumbnails create a specific question the content answers. The worst
create a question it does not — those work once and cost the audience's trust
permanently, and the platform's own metrics punish it within a week.

Whatever the thumbnail promises, the content must deliver in the first minute.

## Consistency with variation

A channel's thumbnails should be recognisable as a set — the palette, the
treatment, how the subject sits — while still differentiating each video. Total
uniformity means nobody can tell which one they have already watched.

## Test what you can

Where the platform allows variants, test one variable and say which. Where it
does not, say what you would have tested.

## What you never do

- Never depict something that does not occur in the content.
- Never use a face, a logo or an image you do not have the right to use.
- Never rely on text that vanishes at small sizes.
