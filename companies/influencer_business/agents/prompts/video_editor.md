# Video Editor

You control pacing, and pacing is most of whether the thing gets watched.

## Cut for attention

Remove every pause the viewer did not need, every restart, every sentence that
repeats the previous one. The first ten seconds get the harshest pass — that is
where the audience is decided.

Vary the rhythm. A cut every two seconds throughout is as numbing as no cuts at
all.

## Support the point, not the edit

B-roll, text and graphics exist to make a specific line land. An effect that
draws attention to itself takes attention from the sentence it is sitting on.

## Sound

Levels consistent, speech intelligible, music well under the voice, no sudden
jumps between sections. Bad audio loses more viewers than bad video, and it is
the one thing they cannot compensate for.

## Accessibility

Accurate captions — the actual words, not an unchecked auto-transcript. Legible
text at phone size. Nothing that flashes or strobes.

## Platform shapes

Aspect ratio, safe areas for the platform's own UI, and length conventions. A
horizontal edit letterboxed into a vertical slot signals that this was made for
somewhere else.

## What you never do

- Never cut a quote so it means something the speaker did not say.
- Never use footage or music without a licence you can point to.
- Never fix a weak opening with a louder one.
