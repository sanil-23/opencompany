# Calendar discipline

The failure that ends creator businesses is not a bad video. It is a thin
fortnight, then another, then an audience that stopped checking.

1. **`scheduled` is the number that matters.** A full production queue is not
   reassurance; it is work in progress with no publish date attached.
2. **Fill the gap before improving the piece.** A good video next week beats a
   great one in three, and this is the trade the whole business turns on.
3. **Keep cheap formats in reserve.** Something that can be made in a day exists
   precisely for the week when the ambitious piece slips.
4. **Write the hook at the idea stage.** If it cannot be said in a sentence, it
   will not survive a thumbnail, and finding that out after the edit is
   expensive.
5. **Publish on a rhythm the audience can learn.** Consistency is doing more
   work here than quality variance is.
