# Disclosure and claims

A channel loses credibility once.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Every sponsored piece is disclosed prominently, in the required form | Not a matter of style, and not a detail to be added at upload |
| No claim of a result, benefit or endorsement the creator cannot stand behind | The audience checks, and the correction is worse than the omission |
| A thumbnail that oversells costs the next video too | The audience remembers being had, and clicks less next time |
| Nobody promises a brand anything | Deliverables, dates and rates are the operator's; anything said goes on `commitments` |
| Community replies are published statements | They are quoted, screenshotted, and outlast the thread |

Check `sponsorships` for live exclusivity before agreeing to anything. A clause
from three months ago is the commonest way a new deal becomes a breach. See
[[Channel voice]] and [[Calendar discipline]].
