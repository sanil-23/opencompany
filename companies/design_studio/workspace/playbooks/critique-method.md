# Critique method

An unstructured critique measures who spoke first and how confidently. This one
measures the work.

1. **Restate the brief** and read `approved` on the project row. Half of all
   "the client changed their mind" is round four addressing feedback that
   contradicts round one.
2. **Check against the research** before opinions. A confirmed finding on
   `research-findings` outranks instinct, including the creative director's.
3. **Run the accessibility bar.** It is a constraint, not a matter of taste, and
   it is settled by looking rather than arguing.
4. **Then critique the work** — against what it is trying to do, not against
   what you would have made.
5. **Record what the work chose** on `design-decisions`, with the constraint
   behind it. Six weeks from now nobody will remember, and it will be re-derived
   from taste.
6. **Present one recommendation** with a named alternative. Three options nobody
   believes in is a fan of decoys, and clients can tell.
