# Evidence and assumptions

What this firm is allowed to present as known.

| Rule | The failure it prevents |
| --- | --- |
| An interview finding is labelled as one | "Three people told us" presented as market fact is how a deck becomes wrong |
| Every model input is an `assumptions` row with its source | An assumption nobody enumerated is one nobody checked |
| `sensitivity` says how far the conclusion moves | Nine assumptions get equal airtime; two of them matter |
| Conditions are stated in the room, not footnoted | A footnote is the same omission with better typography |
| Every recommendation names what it rests on | Otherwise it is a preference, and the executive will treat it as one |

The most useful sentence in any engagement is "this would be a bad idea if
_____". Write it before the workshop, not during it. See the
[[Engagement checklist]] and [[Engagement standards]].
