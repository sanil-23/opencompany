# Scope discipline

The question that sinks consulting firms is not "what work is outstanding" but
"is what we are doing still what we were paid for".

1. **Write what was not sold.** The `scope` field on the engagement has two
   halves and the second one is what prevents the argument.
2. **Record an out-of-scope ask when it is asked** — in `out_of_scope_asks`, on
   the day, in the client's words. Recording it when it becomes a problem is
   recording it too late to be useful.
3. **Saying yes is a decision.** Absorbing a small ask is often right; doing it
   without recording it is how four small asks become an unprofitable
   engagement nobody can account for.
4. **A sponsor's departure changes the engagement.** Move it to `at_risk` the
   day it happens, not when the renewal conversation goes badly.
5. **Anything that commits this firm to deliver something goes on
   `commitments`**, where it has a date somebody can be held to.
