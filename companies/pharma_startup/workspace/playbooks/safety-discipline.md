# Safety discipline

The strictest rule in this organization, and the only one with no exceptions.

1. **Open the signal when it is seen,** not when it is understood. `followed_up
   = "not yet"` is an honest entry and keeps the row open.
2. **Describe what was observed,** with the exposure relative to the intended
   one. A finding at a hundred times the intended exposure and one at the
   intended exposure are different findings.
3. **Nothing advances past an open signal** without the operator saying so
   explicitly.
4. **A signal is never closed as "not pursued".** Explained, mitigated, or the
   program stops — each with the reasoning, read by everyone who touches the
   compound afterwards.
5. **Never soften a signal because a program depends on it.** A signal recorded
   early and followed is a program stopped cheaply; the alternative is what
   makes this industry dangerous rather than merely expensive.

No agent here states a safety, efficacy or dosing conclusion. Those need wet
results and people with the training to interpret them. See
[[Evidence grading]] and the [[Discovery checklist]].
