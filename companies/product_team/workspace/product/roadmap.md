# Roadmap

Themes with bets and an order. Not a list of features with dates.

Owned by `roadmap_planner`; every move is decided by `head_of_product` and
recorded in the `decisions` ledger.

## Now

### Theme: make triage trustworthy

**Bet.** If severity means the same thing every week, the top of the backlog
stops being re-argued and the team can plan a quarter.

**Sequence.** [[Bug triage policy]] adopted → the `bugs` ledger backfilled
from the open reports → first weekly pass reported.

**Depends on.** Nothing. Deliberately first: everything else is planned against
this queue.

## Next

### Theme: know the market on purpose

**Bet.** A dated, sourced competitor register turns "I think they do that" into
something the roadmap can be argued against.

**Sequence.** Register declared → top three competitors read from public sources
→ first teardown posted to the market desk.

**Depends on.** Nothing, but sequenced second because it changes what we
prioritize rather than whether we can prioritize at all.

## Later

Held deliberately empty. A "later" column that fills up is a backlog wearing a
roadmap's clothes — that lives in [[Backlog]].

## Change note

Seeded with the starting order. Every subsequent revision names what moved, what
dropped, and why.
