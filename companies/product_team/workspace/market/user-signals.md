# User signals

Findings, not transcripts. Owned by `user_researcher`.

Each finding says: what users are trying to do, where it breaks, how many say
so, and how strong the signal is.

| Strength | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `strong` | Many independent sources, or few with high-consequence outcomes. |
| `moderate` | A repeated theme across a handful of sources. |
| `weak` | One vivid report. Record it; do not plan against it alone. |

A single loud customer is `weak`, however loud.

## Counter-evidence

Every finding carries its own. A finding that lists only supporting evidence is
advocacy, and it will be planned against as if it were research.

When what arrives is a defect rather than a gap, it goes to triage under
[[Bug triage policy]] — a bug written up as a research finding gets planned
instead of fixed.
