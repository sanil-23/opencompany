# Competitive landscape

What the market already does, with a source and a date on every claim. Owned by
`competitor_analyst`.

## How to read this

Every claim is tagged:

- `observed` — read directly from a public source, which is linked.
- `reported` — somebody else says so, and who is named.
- `inferred` — reasoned to from other facts. Never plan against an inference
  alone.

A claim with no date is a fossil. Re-check anything older than a quarter before
citing it in a prioritization call.
