# Definition of ready

An item is ready when a person who was not in the conversation can pick it up
and know when they have finished.

Four things, all of them present:

1. **The problem** — what is wrong or missing, stated as the user experiences
   it.
2. **The evidence** — why we believe it is real, and how strong the signal is.
3. **The outcome** — what is observably true when it is done. Checkable, not
   aspirational.
4. **The constraints** — what is not negotiable: a compatibility promise, a
   deadline, a surface that must not change.

Anything missing one of these is not ready, however obvious it seems today.
Record which criterion it fails and who can supply the missing piece — "not
ready" without that is a dead end rather than a next step.

Ready is not the same as prioritized. Ready means a priority *can* honestly be
assigned; assigning it is [[Roadmap]] work.
