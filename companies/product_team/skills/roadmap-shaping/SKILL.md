# Roadmap Shaping

A roadmap is themes with bets and an order, not a list of features with dates.

## When to use

- The weekly review, when what is ready has changed.
- Whenever new evidence contradicts the current order.

## Steps

1. **Theme** the ready work: what change in the product is being attempted.
2. **State the bet** for each theme — what has to be true for it to matter.
3. **Sequence** by dependency, then evidence, then reversibility, then cost.
   Cost is a tie-breaker, never the lead.
4. **Date** as ranges, naming what each range depends on.
5. **Diff** it: what moved, what dropped, and the reason for each.

## Output

The sequenced roadmap plus a change note. Every move carries a reason, and a
`decisions` row records it.
