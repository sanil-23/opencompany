# Audience notes

The narrative behind the `audiences` ledger: who this agency's work is aimed at
and how that understanding was arrived at. The ledger holds the rows; this note
holds the reasoning that does not fit in a field.

Two habits worth keeping:

- **Write the objection before the benefit.** What stops somebody buying is more
  useful to a copywriter than what would delight them, and it is the field most
  often left blank.
- **A demographic is not an audience.** "Marketing managers, 30–45" describes a
  bracket. "Somebody who has been told to prove the channel works before next
  quarter" describes a person who can be written to.

An audience with no evidence stays `hypothesised`. It is fine to test against
one; it is not fine to build a quarter on one. See [[Brand voice]] and
[[Measurement standards]].
