# Playtest discipline

Playtesters are the only external truth a studio gets before launch, and the
easiest thing to discount.

1. **Say nothing while they play.** The hardest rule here. Every prompt destroys
   the observation it was about to produce.
2. **Record behaviour, not opinion.** "Six of eight re-opened the map before
   finding the door", never "the tutorial is confusing".
3. **Name the build.** An observation with no build cannot be re-tested, and
   will be argued about instead.
4. **Count.** One player struggling is a quirk; six of eight is a design
   failure, and the difference is the only thing that settles the argument.
5. **Blocking outranks the schedule.** A player who cannot continue is not a
   backlog item.
6. **`accepted` is a real outcome.** Some friction is intended — but it must be
   a decision recorded with its reason, not a memory of having decided it was
   fine.

The designer is the person least able to see the problem, which is the entire
reason this exists. See [[Build honesty]] and the [[Production checklist]].
