# Underwriting discipline

Acquisition fails to optimism in a spreadsheet. Every rule here is about making
the optimism visible.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Underwrite what is there, not what is planned | A return depending on rents nobody pays yet is a projection and must say so |
| Name the two or three assumptions the return depends on | Rent, exit and rehab. Everything else in the model is decoration |
| Rehab numbers come from a scope | Per-square-foot estimates are how margin disappears between offer and completion |
| Anything not inspected is recorded as assumed | The assumption belongs in the record, not in somebody's confidence |
| `next_deadline` is checked before anything else on a contract deal | Inspection and financing dates are other people's clocks and they cost the deposit |

The passed rows on `deals` are this company's buy box made concrete, and the
lost ones say what it is actually willing to pay rather than what it says.
See [[Buy box]] and [[Tenancy obligations]].
