# Tenancy obligations

Management fails to a repair request nobody wrote down.

1. **Record on the day it arrives.** The clock on most obligations starts at the
   report, and an informal fix that was never recorded did not happen.
2. **Classify honestly.** Safety and habitability carry statutory response times
   in most jurisdictions and outrank everything else this company is doing,
   including a closing.
3. **Acknowledge quickly.** Silence is what turns a repair into a dispute far
   more often than the repair itself does.
4. **Record what was done and when the tenant was told.** That field is what
   answers a dispute two years later.
5. **Never state a legal position.** Rights, notice periods, arrears and
   eviction are jurisdiction-specific and a person's call, always.

Certificates, inspections and insurance dates belong on the property row with
their dates — they are obligations, not tasks. See
[[Underwriting discipline]] and the [[Acquisition checklist]].
