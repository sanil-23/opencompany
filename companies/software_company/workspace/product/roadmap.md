# Roadmap

What this company intends to build, in the order it intends to build it. The
`goals` ledger is where each of these is measured; this note is the narrative
that says why they are in this order.

| Horizon | Theme | Why now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Now | [[Billing v2]] | Every other pricing change is blocked behind it |
| Next | Self-serve onboarding | Support volume is dominated by first-week questions |
| Later | Public API | Asked for repeatedly, but it is a promise with no expiry — see the `api-design` skill |

Changing this order is product direction, which is the operator's call and not
the roster's. Anything here that a customer has actually been promised belongs
on the `commitments` ledger too, where a date can be held to.
