# Filing standards

Anything going to a court, registry or regulator is judged on form as much as
substance, and the form requirements are external and unforgiving.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The deadline is on `deadlines` with its source before drafting starts | A date without a source is a guess with a date on it |
| Every factual assertion is checked against the record it cites | A citation that does not support its sentence costs credibility with the only audience that matters |
| Form requirements are read for *this* forum, this time | Forums change their rules, and the last filing may have gone somewhere else |
| Exhibits are ordered as the document refers to them, and attached | The most common rejection is mechanical |
| A licensed human signs, always | This roster prepares filings; it does not file them |

See the [[Intake checklist]] and [[Firm standards]].
