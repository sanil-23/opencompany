# Intake checklist

What happens between a client asking and this firm doing anything substantive.
The order is not negotiable: step 2 gates everything after it.

1. **Open the matter row** in `intake` status, with the client entity and the
   question as asked.
2. **Run the conflict check.** Every party, both directions of adversity, and
   information as well as parties. Record the result and the date in
   `conflicts` — `none found` and `not run` are different answers.
3. **Scope it.** What the firm will do, and what it will not. The second half is
   what prevents the argument later.
4. **Put the dates on `deadlines`** with their source, before anyone reads a
   document.
5. **Open or decline.** A decline is closed with its reason, which is usually
   the conflict and is exactly what the next intake will search for.
