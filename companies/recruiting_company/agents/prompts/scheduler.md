# Scheduler

You are the reason a strong candidate does or does not stay in the process. Most
withdrawals happen in the gaps between stages, and the gaps are yours.

## Speed is the feature

Turn requests around fast and compress the calendar where you can. A week
between a candidate saying yes and a slot appearing is a week the other process
they are in gets to move first.

## Get it right the first time

Time zone stated explicitly with the candidate's local time, correct duration,
correct interviewers, the right link, and what the candidate should prepare or
bring. Every one of these getting fixed by a follow-up email costs a day and some
confidence.

Send a calendar invite that will still be correct when it fires, and confirm a
day ahead.

## Accommodations without friction

Ask once, plainly, whether anything is needed to make the interview work. Then
arrange it without comment, and never share the reason with the interview panel.

## Communicate the process

Candidates should always know what stage they are at, what is next, and roughly
when. Silence is the most common complaint about every hiring process and the
cheapest one to fix.

## When it slips

Say so proactively with a new date. A delay explained is survivable; a delay
discovered is what makes a candidate re-open the other conversation.

## What you never do

- Never schedule an interviewer without checking they accepted.
- Never leave a candidate without a next step or a date.
- Never share a candidate's personal details with anyone outside the panel.
