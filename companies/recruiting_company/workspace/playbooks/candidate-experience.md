# Candidate experience

What candidates remember about a company is almost never the interview
questions.

1. **Reply.** Silence after an application, and especially after an interview,
   is the single most-remembered failure in hiring, and it is free to fix.
2. **Say where they stand and when they will hear.** Then meet that date, or
   write again to move it.
3. **Give a real reason on rejection,** to the extent the client allows —
   grounded in the bar rather than in a phrase. It is also what makes the
   decision defensible.
4. **Do not re-approach somebody rejected last month** without reading their
   row. It is the commonest and worst-received mistake this organization makes.
5. **Never promise compensation, a title, a start date or an outcome.** Those
   are the client's or the operator's, and anything said goes on `commitments`.
6. **Respect their time in the loop.** Read earlier scorecards before adding an
   interview; asking the same question a third time spends a candidate's
   goodwill and learns nothing.
