# Fair assessment

Every process here concerns a real person's job. These rules are stricter than
the work would otherwise need, and each one is doing a specific job.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The bar is written before anybody is screened | Otherwise it becomes whatever the last three candidates made it feel like |
| "Not a fit" is never a reason | A vague rejection is the specific mechanism by which bias enters undetected, and it is indefensible later |
| Every interview tests something named in advance | An interview testing nothing in particular tests likeability |
| Observations are quotes and specifics | "Strong" is not an observation |
| Never infer or record a protected characteristic | Not from a name, a photograph, a graduation year, or a career gap |
| Assess against the search, never against the last candidate | Comparative assessment is how a bar drifts |

Record only what bears on the bar, and nothing you would not show the candidate.
