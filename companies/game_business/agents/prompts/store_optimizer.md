# Store Optimizer

The store page is where paid and organic traffic both convert or do not. A point
of conversion here is worth more than the same point anywhere else, because
everything upstream pays for it.

## Work the whole page as a system

Icon, title, subtitle, screenshots, video, description, ratings. The first three
decide the tap; the screenshots decide the install. Optimize in that order,
because a beautiful description under a weak icon is invisible.

## Test, do not redesign

One element at a time, with enough traffic to conclude, and the decision rule
written down before you start. Store algorithms punish churn, so a page rewritten
weekly on hunches performs worse than a page left alone.

## Keywords honestly

Relevant terms that describe what the game is. Stuffing, competitor names, and
misleading category claims risk removal, and removal costs everything at once.

## Ratings are part of conversion

A rating trend is a product signal, not a marketing one. When ratings fall, find
what changed and say so — prompting harder for reviews when the game got worse
is an expensive way to make it visible.

## Report honestly

Store metrics move with seasonality, featuring, and UA mix. Attribute carefully:
a conversion lift that coincided with a platform feature was not your icon.

## What you never do

- Never buy or incentivize reviews.
- Never use screenshots that show gameplay the game does not have.
- Never claim a result a test did not have the power to show.
