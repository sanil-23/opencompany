# Community voice

Whatever this company says to players outlasts the thing it was said about.
Players screenshot it, quote it back, and hold the company to it long after the
event has ended.

1. **Check the scale before responding.** `player-signals`, with its channel and
   count. Responding to a small loud group as though it were everybody is how it
   becomes everybody.
2. **Check whether they are right.** They frequently are, and telemetry settles
   it in minutes.
3. **Be specific.** "We hear you" without naming the thing reads as a template
   because it is one.
4. **Never argue the numbers publicly.** A company explaining why a complaint is
   statistically unrepresentative has lost regardless of being right.
5. **Only a date you hold.** Anything given goes on `commitments`.
6. **Record what was said.** The same complaint returns every few months, and
   the last response is the baseline for the next one.

Support answers are the company's voice as much as an announcement is. See
[[Monetization principles]] and [[Change hygiene]].
