# Evidence standards

What this lab is allowed to treat as established.

| Rule | The failure it prevents |
| --- | --- |
| The librarian fetches and never reads; the scholar reads and never fetches | A search snippet reported as a source somebody read |
| `kind` is primary, secondary or commentary, honestly | Most evidential disagreements are really disagreements about this |
| `establishes` is written narrowly — the cohort, the period, the sample | A conclusion cited far past what it supports |
| Every finding names what would falsify it | A claim nothing could disprove is not a finding, however true it sounds |
| Contradictions are recorded, never smoothed | Two sources that disagree are the most informative thing available that day |
| An absence of evidence is reported as a result | An absence quietly omitted is a fabrication with extra steps |

A finding reaches `defended` by surviving the critic, not by nobody having
looked. See the [[Investigation loop]].
