# Open questions

The narrative behind the `questions` ledger — what the lab is currently trying
to find out and why those lines and not others. The ledger holds the rows and
their state; this note holds the argument for the shape of the investigation.

Worth writing down each time a line closes:

- **Why it was the wrong question,** if it was. That is the most reusable thing
  a closed line produces, and the least likely to be recorded.
- **What the dead end cost.** A line abandoned after two sources and one
  abandoned after thirty are different warnings.

A question already tried and abandoned is the cheapest mistake available here,
and this note plus the closed rows on `questions` are the only things that
prevent it. See [[Investigation loop]].
